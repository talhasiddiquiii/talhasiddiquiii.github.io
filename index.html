About

I studied Organisational Psychology because I wanted to understand why people work the way they do. Since then, the work I've enjoyed most has been finding out why something isn't working and fixing it with the people involved: cutting a proposal process's turnaround by 68%, reducing client onboarding time by 48% by changing how two teams planned together, and designing sales incentive policies that showed me how much the way a role is set up shapes how people perform in it.

Before that, I ran focus groups with Ipsos and learnt that what people say first is rarely the real reason behind what they do. Today I work across supermarket and hardware stores in Perth, where I watch closely how frontline work actually runs. I'm building towards organisational development, workforce design and consulting, with a particular interest in frontline and shift-based workers.
